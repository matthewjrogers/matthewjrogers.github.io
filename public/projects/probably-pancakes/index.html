probably-pancakes

R

A machine learning app that answers the only question that matters: is this recipe pancakes? Trained a Random Forest classifier on 3,200 recipes scraped from the web, then wrapped it in a polished Shiny app. Born from a household mystery — a scrap of paper labeled ‘probably pancakes.’
